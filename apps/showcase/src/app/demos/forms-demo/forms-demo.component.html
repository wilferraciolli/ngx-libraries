Date & Time Fields

Instant date-time stores an exact moment as a UTC instant (YYYY-MM-DDThh:mm:ssZ) and edits it in a timezone. Business date (YYYY-MM-DD) and business time (HH:mm) ignore timezones entirely. All three display in the chosen locale, not the browser's.

About Dynamic Forms

The DynamicForm component provides a configuration-driven approach to building forms:

  • Schema-based: Define form structure with an array of field definitions
  • Type-safe: Full TypeScript support with generics extending BaseSchema
  • Flexible: Supports text, email, password, search, radio, select, checkbox, number, range, textarea, code, business date, business time, and instant date-time fields
  • Validation: Built-in required, minLength/maxLength, min/max, email, pattern and date/time range rules with inline error display
  • Signals-based: Built on Angular's experimental Signals Forms API (@angular/forms/signals)

This approach reduces boilerplate and makes forms more maintainable and testable.
